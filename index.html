How this is calculated

    Squiggle publishes no premiership probability, so this number is derived rather than quoted. Legs marked fitted use a conversion trained on Squiggle's own published margins this season — they are the least certain part of the number. The Grand Final is priced as a neutral ground.

    This site argues a case. It leads with the numbers that support Geelong rather than the bleakest way of stating the same data — the compound probability of winning every remaining game is the figure above, and it is smaller. What it will not do is bend one: every figure on this page is the real one, computed from the source on each refresh, and a claim that stops being true stops being shown.
